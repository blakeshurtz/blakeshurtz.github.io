Voice to AI

October 25, 2026

I haven’t started using dots yet. Apparently you can use your voice to design a rocketship and build it in Blender. OK, so what is. It’s the voices API plus an integration, perhaps an MCP server. I’m not there yet. I’ve tried voice before with mixed results. I think there’s a lot of potential, but we’re going to see a lot of weirdness as we learn how to build. I’m seeing this on Reddit with AI job interviewers. These will get better of course, but they’re bad and condescending as hell. They deserve to be pilloried. It should raise the question of how AI will change job interviews. I would go with the Black Mirror interpretation. Everyone will be wearing Meta-style glasses that analyze your interlocutor’s biological parameters, think Apple Watch plays poker. This makes sense, while at the same time it would erode trust.

This is all to say that I’ve settled into talking with the latest voice API: GPT Live-1. Live-1 seems to be able to do something that is really necessary but I haven’t seen until now. It can talk at difference cadences. It can slow down. It is also uses more natural “filler language”, not ums and likes but language that overall makes its speech less dense and therefore easier to follow.

So now I’m talking to an AI that sounds human enough (Arbor), that talks like a person and I can interrupt them anytime. So how do I use it? As a replacement for reading my screen because, heck, it’s a beautiful summer day and I don’t want to stare into my phone and read the awesome 9500 word article that I had Deep Research produce on the recent history of modern quantitative economics. Which, by the way, is a necessary pre-requisite to actually have a good research-oriented conversation. Live-1 is agentic. It will pause to think, reason, search the internet, all while I inhabit a silence that has moved from uncomfortable (“is this thing working?”) to expected. I want a well thought out answer after all.

But what’s most important is that it can read and recall the entire deep research article. This allows me to put my phone down and stare off into space, although the screen needs to remain on, while intentionally having one heck of an erudite conversation. It works well. It feels a bit like the way it feels to read Plato. There is a deliberate investigation. We are speaking a framework into existence. The AI can take me down avenues of thought while we can always return to the main thread. I set out some basic expectations for the AI to follow. One step at a time. Let’s use an example. It pushes back against my definitions and logical structure. This is feels like what ChatGPT was designed to do.
